## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Reads as a well-executed generic government template with Indonesian judicial vocabulary poured in, not a design conceived from MA's specific institutional character. Correct legal terminology shows research, but hero imagery is generic stock, news photos are unrelated Unsplash stock, and the icon-circle-plus-card pattern is what any civic template produces.

**Deterministic scan:** detect.mjs found 3 CLI-level side-tab findings in Navbar.tsx (lines 106, 533, 705) — border-left accents on nav/dropdown items that turn gold on hover/active. Judged a defensible interactive-affordance pattern, not the canonical "AI slop" tell.

**Browser overlay evidence** (5 pages, then torn down): 18-32 findings per page, dominated by low-contrast (7-16/page — e.g. #c9a84c on #ffffff at 2.3:1, #8a8a8a on #ffffff at 3.5:1, under the 4.5:1 minimum), undersized-ui-text (10px "Republik Indonesia" on every page), nested-cards (18x on /faq), skipped-heading (h1→h3 on 3/5 pages), line-length (144 chars/line on /id/berita). Strong agreement with the LLM review's Sam persona flag on --ma-text-muted contrast.

## Overall Impression

The visual system is more coherent than most first-pass redesigns, and real research went into MA's terminology. But the site fails at the two things a judicial-services portal cannot afford: case search returns fabricated/wrong results, and the entire "Informasi" section is unreachable on mobile. Both assessments converge on pervasive contrast failures. Fix search logic and the mobile IA gap before investing further in polish.

## What's Working

- FAQPage.tsx — categorized accordion plus 8-term legal glossary (Kasasi, Banding, PK, Verstek) is real domain-specific content.
- src/styles/enhanced.css — coherent, well-named token system applied consistently across Homepage, SearchPage, Footer.
- SearchPage.tsx's EventDetailModal — print-friendly modal matching real registrar/citizen workflow needs.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Case search returns fabricated/wrong results**
- Why it matters: SearchPage.tsx:1234-1236 ignores the keyword and returns the full static mock list for any truthy input — searching "Pidana" returns Perdata/TUN/Agama cases labeled "5 hasil untuk 'Pidana'." Core trust feature showing confidently wrong results.
- Fix: Filter mock results by substring match at minimum; blocking before real backend integration.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P0] Mobile navigation drops the entire "Informasi" section**
- Why it matters: Navbar.tsx:687-711 only lists Beranda/Perkara/Bantuan on mobile; informasiItems dropdown never renders on mobile. Confirmed live at 390x844.
- Fix: Render the same informasiItems list inside the mobile menu.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P1] Homepage's "Cek Status Perkara" CTA silently breaks its own promise**
- Why it matters: Homepage.tsx:35 navigates to /perkara?tab=status, but SearchPage.tsx never reads the tab param.
- Fix: Implement the status tab, or stop promising it from the homepage.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] Widespread text-contrast failures, detector-confirmed on every page**
- Why it matters: 7-16 low-contrast findings per page, all under WCAG AA's 4.5:1 minimum. Systemic across the token palette.
- Fix: Darken --ma-text-muted and gold-on-light-background pairings; formal contrast pass across tokens.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P1] Dead/orphaned code shipped alongside production routes**
- Why it matters: Footer.tsx:82 links to /tentang (404s for real visitors); Navbar.tsx's tentangItems never rendered; DigitalMenuDropdown.tsx and JadwalSidang.tsx fully built but never routed.
- Fix: Wire these up or delete them before this goes past prototype stage.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Clicks "Cek Status Perkara" expecting a status check, lands on generic search (dropped ?tab=status). Gets back wrong-type cases with a confident result count. Calendar shows "Tidak ada agenda" on today's date since mock events are all May-June 2026.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**: Desktop dropdowns open only via onMouseEnter/onMouseLeave, no confirmed keyboard-only path. Mini-calendar encodes event type only as a colored dot. --ma-text-muted contrast likely fails WCAG AA (detector-confirmed).

**Casey (Mobile)**: Cannot reach /id/artikel, /id/berita, /id/keputusan, /id/pengumuman, or /profil from the mobile menu at all. Hero takes 2+ full screens of scroll before real content. /perkara stacks search, filters, calendar, and agenda grid into one long column.

## Minor Observations

- FAQ's "Semua" tab dumps all 23 questions unpaginated; 18 detector nested-cards findings on /faq.
- Several groupings exceed the ≤4-items-per-decision-point guideline (8 service cards, 6-option filters); 4 of 8 cognitive-load checklist items fail, concentrated on /perkara.

- /wireframe* routes are mounted in the same production router as the real site.
- backgroundAttachment: "fixed" on the hero is a jank/perf risk on mobile Safari.
